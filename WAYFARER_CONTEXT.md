## Product direction

Wayfarer is a standalone, browser-based D&D 5e game for **one to four people playing without a DM**. The intended loop is to choose a published adventure, bring or build characters, open a shareable room, and play together. The engine presents the adventure's story as the party explores, runs hostile creatures, and resolves rolls, combat, spell slots, class resources, and results. The grid and the room's saved state are central to the experience. Support for arbitrary modules, classes, or spells is a goal with varying coverage today.

The user likes Baldur's Gate 3 as a reference for how movement, actions, bonus actions, spell slots, class abilities, rests, and combat feedback feel. Wayfarer uses its own interface on 5-foot grids. Favor faithful D&D 5e dice, weapon damage, items, and spell mechanics, with deliberate game-specific rules called out when they differ. Selective Foundry data imports and animation assets fit the standalone direction. Requiring each player to set up Foundry or trying to run every extension was judged too messy.

The dashboard is for campaign setup, character sheets, the builder, adventure browsing, profile, and the spell rules editor. Play opens a separate, map-first screen. Keep the action dock compact and bottom-aligned, and let character sheets and tools open as needed. The visual baseline uses **Spectral** for titles and character names and **Alegreya Sans** for body text, with original source artwork. The user explicitly asked for zero AI-generated art, including dashboard art.

## Architecture and development

The app uses vanilla ES modules, HTML, and CSS. The editable web source lives directly under `dist/`. The build copies deployable assets and modules into `dist/client/`, including the nested spell library, and bundles the Pages Worker as `dist/client/_worker.js`. It also emits `dist/server/` for Worker development. Edit the source files, then rebuild the generated outputs. The build checks bundled raster artwork and animation hashes. It copies the browser spell schema into the Worker so they validate the same rules. [Build script](scripts/build.mjs)

## Accounts, multiplayer, and saved rooms

- Guests can join via an invite and choose a username. The session shows players and character claims. Single-click a controllable portrait to select, and double-click to open the sheet. Portraits claimed by another player remain inspectable. Party controls add characters mid-combat, seed a valid nearby position, and preserve initiative already in progress. Profile is accessed through the top right. Uploaded profile photos can identify players in chat or a room when they have no character portrait. [Game screen](dist/game-screen.mjs) · [Chat](dist/game-chat.mjs) · [Profile](dist/profile-photo.mjs)
- Google Identity Services is wired into the Pages Worker and D1 accounts. The browser uses the public `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` configured in Pages, with the production URL in Google's authorized JavaScript origins. The Worker verifies Google credentials and establishes its own session. Accounts sync characters, token images, spell rules, profile details, and linked rooms across devices. Guest data can be imported on first sign-in, and conflicts are surfaced in Profile. The 5etools ZIP, drafts, and optionally remembered Gemini key stay in the browser. Earlier setup included saving the client ID in Cloudflare. A fresh end-to-end Google sign-in was outside the most recent release smoke test. [Account client](dist/account-client.mjs) · [Account Worker](worker/accounts.mjs) · [Migrations](drizzle/)
- Room IDs are the shareable entry points. A room has **one canonical battle ledger across its adventure**, even when it spans several maps. HP, slots, inventory, XP, character identities, NPC state, and story belong to that room. NPCs use a `sceneKey`; map records keep physical details such as walls, terrain, arrivals, markers, exploration, and zones. Switching maps projects the active scene from the ledger. Multiple rooms can use the same adventure independently. The room-open migration enriches old saves while preserving party and encounter progress. [Room state](dist/room-state.mjs) · [Room upgrades](dist/room-upgrades.mjs)
- D1 stores room saves, membership, chat, and account data. R2 handles images when bound, with chunked D1 image storage available as a fallback. Writes use revisions and clients poll for changes. Keep valid simultaneous actions independent, and retry a conflict only while the relevant actor and budget still match. Unpublished room state is never a safe place for credentials. [Multiplayer client](dist/multiplayer.mjs) · [Session Worker](worker/session.mjs)
- Room deletion is creator-only. Authorization uses a private creator cookie checked against a server-side hash, with account ownership where linked. The public player/controller ID, invite, and revision do not grant deletion rights. The revision protects against stale confirmation. Legacy rooms without verified ownership may be undeletable through the UI. An earlier request named five legacy room cards for deletion. I have no verified completion record for that historical cleanup. Match exact room IDs before any future deletion. [Room owner code](worker/room-owner.mjs) · [Ownership tests](scripts/test-room-owner-security.mjs)

## Maps, fog, and play presentation

- Use the clean player map where paired with a DM map. The DM metadata still supplies room regions and encounter context. Existing printed grids should determine alignment where possible, with manual correction available. Source creature artwork and footprint drive token placement, collision, reach, and targeting. Medium creatures occupy one square, Large two by two, and Huge three by three. Uploaded character tokens should follow the sheet into all rooms that use it. [Map grid](dist/map-grid.mjs) · [Monster artwork](dist/monster-art.mjs) · [Token sync](dist/token-room-sync.mjs)
- Camera panning and zoom are distinct gestures. Keep source raster quality on zoom and reuse map backgrounds during animation. Selected and hovered tokens use a thin artwork-following outline. Movement previews shade route squares and highlight the destination with a character name and cost. Blocked empty destinations show “Can't move here,” with detailed feedback in the board status. The resource chips still scroll horizontally with the white scrollbar hidden. The idle map name and artwork text are gone from the overlay. Artwork details live in Tools. Action feedback remains on the board. [Camera](dist/map-camera.mjs) · [Grid renderer](dist/grid-view.mjs)
- The manual wall editor supports off-grid vertices, snapping, chained segments, dragging, doors, stop/resume, undo, and redo. Wall traces show while editing. Fog supports personal or party vision, token vision settings, explored memory, an on/off control, and softened edges. A prior temporary fog disable was superseded by the current feature. `game-settings.mjs` currently defaults fog on, with room-level settings controlling actual play. [Wall editor](dist/wall-editor.mjs) · [Dynamic fog](dist/dynamic-fog.mjs) · [Default setting](dist/game-settings.mjs)
- The preferred baseline for encounters and maps is deterministic. Baked floor/wall/door detection produces draft geometry for supported modules, applies when hand-authored walls are absent, and leaves movement blocking off by default for inferred data. Gemini is optional and limited to a draft wall forecast. A successful forecast previously produced poor vertices, so visual review matters. Forecast code includes image crops, geometry cleanup, retry/backoff for transient errors, optional alternate model, cancellation, review before apply, and a same-origin fallback. Model availability and cost require fresh verification if revisited. [Baked walls](dist/baked-walls.mjs) · [Forecast pipeline](dist/ai-forecast.mjs) · [Forecast review](dist/forecast-review.mjs)
- Story discovery uses authored room entries and journal events as players explore. Some named interactions have bespoke logic. Broad automation of plot branches, dialogue, and every module encounter remains a product goal. [Story journal](dist/adventure-story.mjs)
- Chat and activity panels default to the right and support independent collapse, moving, and resizing. The activity log can appear separately, be hidden, or enter the same chat timeline as system messages. Keep party portraits fixed and preserve scroll position. Story and Tools belong at the top-right. In-game sheets close when clicked outside, while persistent panels stay open. [Floating panels](dist/floating-panels.mjs) · [Game chat](dist/game-chat.mjs) · [Modal dismissal](dist/modal-dismiss.mjs)

## Combat, resources, and world interactions

- Free movement during exploration. Enter combat when hostiles detect or are engaged by the party. Escape can end it. Use WASD or arrows with a selected token, and Space to end a turn. Diagonals cost one square, with equal-cost paths preferring straight cardinal steps. Combat previews show the remaining movement budget. The engine handles hostile turns and reactions. Eligible consecutive party members can share a turn window with separate budgets for movement, action, bonus action, and follow-ups. Hostile initiative separates groups. [Combat](dist/combat.mjs) · [Enemy turns](dist/enemy-turn.mjs) · [Shared-turn tests](scripts/test-shared-turns.mjs)
- First usable weapon and nearest enemy are useful defaults. Sneak Attack requires an eligible finesse/ranged weapon and advantage or a qualifying ally near the target, with net disadvantage blocking it. Default critical hits use the kept natural 20; nearby condition-based auto-crits are opt-in. Hestia's Fury is finesse, grants Pebbly fire immunity while equipped through its item rules, provides at-will Fireball, and grants three Wall of Fire uses per long rest independent of wizard slots. Azuros' naginata resolves to a glaive-style attack animation. [Hestia's Fury](dist/hestias-fury.mjs) · [Attack visuals](dist/attack-visuals.mjs)
- Committed action events feed both player and monster roll messages, logs, token callouts, and dice. The dice use the actual die types and a full-screen overhead scene. Damage is committed to authoritative state during resolution, with the displayed HP changing after the animation reveals the result. Preserve immediate movement feedback and avoid replayed defeat callouts. Keep defeated tokens on the board with a visible marker, and display numeric HP and bars. [Action events](dist/action-events.mjs) · [Dice scene](dist/dice-scene.mjs) · [HP presentation](dist/health-presentation.mjs)
- Interact opens one context menu for loot, helping, picking up, placing, throwing, and other applicable actions. Loot comes from explicit monster equipment or map treasure and updates the room sheet. CR XP is awarded once and split among initiative participants, with the finisher receiving an additional 20% of their own share, rounded down. Help can bring an eligible downed party member to 1 HP. Healing potions can be thrown. Dead and unconscious bodies remain available for carrying and placement within the relevant limits. [World interactions](dist/world-interactions.mjs) · [XP](dist/encounter-rewards.mjs)
- Two short rests per long rest, outside combat. A rest in a location with an applicable authored encounter table can be interrupted. One-hour and eight-hour attempts use the relevant table cadence and check conditions, so a long rest can expose the party to more checks. [Dungeon rests](dist/dungeon-rests.mjs)

## Characters, spell automation, and assets

- Character sheets support workbook and public Google Sheets import, editable stats and inventory, uploaded tokens, and a guided builder. Class, subclass, race/species, background, feats, proficiencies, languages, equipment, spells, and slot suggestions use the local 5etools data. The builder covers single-class progression through level 20. Imported multiclass characters have separate derivation paths. The app surfaces source options and provides executable controls for implemented features. A populated feature description alone is not proof that its effects are automated. Azuros, the user's level 9 monk example, is a useful check for class choices, Ki, bonus actions, and follow-ups. Search suggestions should cover relevant form fields across the app. [Character rules](dist/character-rules.mjs) · [Class feature view](dist/class-feature-view.mjs) · [Character builder](dist/character-builder.mjs) · [Suggestions](dist/search-suggestions.mjs)
- Spell rules v2 has **174 built-in definitions** across cantrips and levels 1–5 (PHB, TCE, and XGE 2014 combat spells). Match canonical spell references (`phb|…`, `tce|…`, `xge|…`), check the edition, and verify casting through the real hotbar. Enabled user rules take priority. Built-ins live in code and are not copied into room rule snapshots. The dashboard editor handles custom definitions, a private animated test board, imports, and publishing saved overrides to rooms. Some editor paths still need v2 coverage. Ally targets are valid where the rules permit them. Fireball and other point spells accept an empty square, lines and cones use a direction click, and drawn walls use two endpoints. [Built-ins](dist/spell-library/index.mjs) · [XGE batch](dist/spell-library/batch-xge.mjs) · [Rule store](dist/spell-rule-store.mjs) · [Rule editor](dist/spell-rule-editor.mjs) · [Spell shapes](dist/spell-shapes.mjs)
- The engine covers slots and upcasting, scaling, concentration, attack and save rolls, reactions, areas and persistent zones, weapon-linked casts, forced movement, conditions, and some summons. Shield can trigger against Magic Missile and blocks all its damage while active. A failed Counterspell attempt still uses the reaction and slot. Hypnotic Pattern ends on damage or a nearby creature's wake action. Dominate Person and Beast expose a Command tile under Spells. Free orders can be sent off-turn. Total control spends an action and expires at the end of the caster's next turn. The enemy-turn handler follows orders and defaults to Dodge. Losing concentration or passing the damage-triggered Wisdom save clears domination and its orders. The release smoke test verified the free command path in live play; the suite covers the total-control action cost, thrall attacks, and order cleanup at engine level. [Spell resolver](dist/custom-spells.mjs) · [Reactions](dist/reactions.mjs) · [Dominate](dist/dominate.mjs)
- Foundry import is selective and data-oriented. It can inspect spell JSON and text compendia, bind recognized rules, and leave unsupported material as disabled drafts. Foundry module scripts do not run inside Wayfarer. The linked 5e Complete V11 compendium contains records and automation metadata of varying depth. Importing a description alone does not add runtime behavior. [Foundry importer](dist/foundry-spell-import.mjs)
- Free JB2A animation assets and a pinned animation index supply spell visuals. Automated Animations and 5e Animations are reference projects. Combat animation code maps supported simple and martial weapons and chooses suitable projectile clips by distance, adjusting flight timing and preserving the impact. Damage cues and cast visuals are presentation features with fallbacks. Boss Loot was explored as another asset source; verify each asset's recorded origin and rights before reuse. Preserve asset credits and license terms if the project becomes commercial. Keep the user's no-generated-art requirement separate from optional Gemini geometry detection. [Animation credits](dist/assets/animations/credits.json) · [Spell animation lookup](dist/spell-animation-library.mjs) · [Damage effects](dist/damage-effects.mjs)

## What to verify next

- Exercise representative new spells from fresh and existing rooms, with paired browsers for action ownership, reactive spells, character uploads, tokens, and snapshots. The tests cover common primitives; they do not establish complete rule fidelity for all 129 spells.
- Review advanced or partial spell behavior as it comes up. The spell guide lists remaining cases such as weapon-cantrip secondary effects, complex summons and sustained spells, higher-level automation, and editor coverage. Cross-check 2014 and 2024 variants with the local source record before treating name matches as equivalent.
- Measure movement and map panning on large real rooms. Review inferred wall overlays by module, especially doors and larger maps. Keep manual adjustments and undo available.
- Keep dashboard/UI changes separate from broad engine releases when the broader working tree is unverified. Build from a known snapshot, test the built browser entry, verify production, and clean up disposable QA rooms. For source changes, check the exact Git diff and avoid depending on a local archive, temporary test directory, or credential in the published build.
